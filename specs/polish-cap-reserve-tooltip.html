04 / Does the evidence measure the stated problem?

The connection is explicit.
The cap calculation is still missing.

The reports justify export caps partly through insufficient upward balancing capacity. Our comparison measures that procurement gap—and its required total matches a separately published PSE reserve margin.

DOCUMENTED RATIONALE

1. Export caps protect upward reserves

“maintaining the required upward balancing capacity”

URE identifies this, alongside meeting domestic demand, as the purpose of Polish export constraints. It also says separate pre-day-ahead procurement helped reduce their activation.

URE, 30 Dec 2025 · “Purpose of allocation constraints” and “Impact of the balancing market reform”

2. The TSOs plot the same kind of gap

“Scarcity of total upward reserve offers in primary procurement (volume procured - volume required).”

Figure 1 is sourced to PSE’s reporting portal. The surrounding text describes basic procurement at 08:30 on D−1, explains that bought capacity should leave the day-ahead offer pool, and attributes remaining constraints to insufficient market liquidity. This is a Core TSO explanatory document hosted by ACER, not an ACER finding.

3. PSE publishes the two sides

The endpoint map labels mbp-tp “Wielkości mocy bilansujących nabytych w trybie podstawowym” (our translation: balancing capacity purchased in the basic process). The portal labels wmb “Required balancing power amounts”.

Purchased ↑ MWfcr_g + afrr_g + mfrrd_g + rr_g
Required ↑ MWfcrg + afrrg + frrg + rrg

The additional FRR requirement is above aFRR; the sum does not count aFRR twice. This identifies the relevant reports and products. The TSO figure does not list API fields or its underlying rows: we have not reproduced that historical figure exactly.

What we can testDid the published basic purchases cover the published upward requirement before the normal submission target? This is directly relevant to the stated procurement-shortfall rationale. It is not the entire security justification.

How to reproduce the check Queries, joins and publication-version limits

On 14 Nov 2026, retrieve https://api.raporty.pse.pl/api/pk5l-wp for each date above with $filter=business_date eq 'YYYY-MM-DD' and $first=1000; follow pagination. Join wmb.dtime_utc to pk5l-wp.plan_dtime_utc one-to-one: both are hour-end stamps. Sum all four non-null upward requirement fields, subtract req_pow_res, and count exact zero differences. Join mbp-tp.dtime_utc in the same way for purchases. Do not multiply hours into MTUs.

The requirement and purchase records are the existing research collection’s cached versions; their per-file retrieval dates were not recorded. The planning records fetched on 14 Nov are later versions, published on each delivery day. Do not use these late planning records as pre-10:30 evidence. Refetching may return revisions. A separately retained 12 Nov evening version of the 13 Nov plan also matched all 24 requirement sums.

At 19:00 on 13 Nov, the newly retrieved plan shows net grid demand of 22,461 MW. Nine per cent is 2,021.49 MW, not 2,347 MW: a 325.51 MW difference. This is not a mismatch between reserve products and the margin. PSE defines the D/D+1 margin against demand to be covered by domestic plants, including plant own-use and net exchanges, rather than net grid demand alone. The implied denominator is 26,077.78 MW; we have not reconstructed every component of that denominator from this response.

What the other reports do—and do not—establish

ACER’s premise needs reconciliation

“there is no dedicated procurement of balancing capacity prior to the day-ahead market.”

Paragraph 168 attributes the explanation to the Polish TSO. Taken literally, this conflicts with URE’s reform account and the TSOs’ description of morning procurement. It does not validate our field mapping or establish that procurement meets every operational need.

ACER 2026 market-integration report · section 2.4.4, paragraph 168, printed p. 60

The quarterly simulation tests a different quantity

“comparing the historical reserve levels and the simulated reserve levels (in absence of AC)”

PSE’s Q4 2025 assessment, slide 2, tests operational reserve levels after removing both cap directions. It uses a 1,000 MW upward intervention threshold, explicitly below its stated 9% requirement, and 500 MW downward. Its renewable-curtailment result is predominantly downward-reserve evidence. These thresholds are not our morning procurement totals.

PSE Q4 2025 assessment · slides 2 and 7, in JAO’s quarterly archive
The remaining missing linkThe summed requirement matches PSE’s reported reserve margin in every sampled hour. We still lack the original, timestamped cap-calculation inputs showing that this exact margin/version was used, plus evidence of deliverability and feasible alternatives. A procurement match challenges a simple “not enough bought” explanation; it does not prove the export cap was unnecessary.
Implementation contract Fetch boundaries, schemas & refresh policy

Baseline

The daily publisher fetches market inputs, constructs a typed day bundle, writes immutable interval objects, and advertises a complete generation through its manifest. MarketInterval.caps contains inferred binding cap effects; the frontend's pages/cnec/day-figure.ts renders the outline tooltip. Neither the production models nor publisher fetch PSE reserves as of this spec. The research generator on the investigation branch is evidence for the source adapter, not a runtime dependency.

Design

  • Add coppersushi/pipelines/cnec/data_sources/pse.py for HTTP, pagination, response validation and response retrieval timestamps. Fetch wmb and mbp-tp once per delivery day from bundles.prepare, using the source contract. No browser-to-PSE requests, no separate hover service, no supplementary purchases.
  • Keep version selection, product totals and hourly-to-quarter-hour alignment in a pure cnec/reserves.py transform, accepting validated models. Follow the time rules. Reuse MarketDay for the UTC interval grid and D−1 deadline. The research script rejects duplicate hours; production must select eligible versions explicitly rather than copy that restriction.
  • Add an interval-level polish_upward_reserves contract in cnec/models.py, separate from the binding-only caps list. It carries independent required and purchased observations: total MW, product components, source publication instant, and availability status. Preserve valid zero values. Distinguish missing records, incomplete products, no eligible pre-submission version, ambiguous versions and fetch failure. A later excluded publication time may be retained as diagnostic evidence, but never labelled as the timestamp of an eligible value.
  • Join by UTC delivery start during bundle construction. Serialize the optional evidence into the same immutable interval object. Its content participates in the generation hash. Regenerate Python/TypeScript contracts together and version the publication contract if the strict existing reader cannot accept the added fields. The browser fetches no extra object on hover and cannot mix generations.
  • Format the two publication instants in Europe/Brussels at the presentation boundary. The export-cap tooltip retains exact cap MW and existing price attribution, then shows “Required upward capacity” and “Purchased before submission target”, each with MW and its own full publication date/time. Use the same content for desktop hover and touch selection. Missing values get a brief reason. A short note says matching totals does not establish that the cap was unnecessary. Do not present upward evidence as justification of an import cap; downward procurement is out of scope.

PSE is optional contextual evidence: an outage or invalid reserve response must not suppress otherwise valid market data. Publish explicit unavailable status and retain diagnostics in publisher logs. Already-published days are skipped by the scheduler; missing enrichment therefore needs an explicit refresh/backfill using the existing publication path, not an assumption that the next scheduled run will fill it. No new polling service in the first implementation. Record retrieval times and use eligible cached records when available; refetching may return revisions. Historical raw-response archiving is not required. Never carry a value over from another delivery day.
