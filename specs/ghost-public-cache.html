Ghost / cache design · implementation in progress

Keep public articles readable.

Planned behavior: Cloudflare serves public pages for five minutes and may reuse the last good copy for 24 hours when Ghost fails. Admin, previews and personalized responses always reach Ghost.

Two independent protections

#284 restores Ghost after database maintenance. The cache protects readers during that recovery. Both remain useful.

Acceptance

  • Anonymous public HTML becomes a cache hit; browser freshness stays short.
  • A cached page remains readable during a simulated 502; errors never replace the last good page.
  • Admin, APIs, previews, query variants and sessions bypass HTML caching; private and Set-Cookie responses are never shared.
  • Published edits, changed slugs, unpublishing, theme and settings changes invalidate affected public HTML.
  • Images and unrelated app hosts are not purged.
  • Automated checks cover the cache boundary and purge failures.
Implementation and remaining rollout

Cloudflare Cache Rules plus CDN-specific origin headers. A signed Ghost site.changed webhook hard-purges the public HTML tag. Astra Goldfish review confirmed coverage of publishing, unpublishing, scheduled publication, themes and settings. Best effort is intentional: no queue or retry service.

Stale content is best effort: eviction, purges and cold locations can leave no copy. Caching is not an archive. A missed webhook can leave stale content, including an unpublished page, through the outage window. Ghost and Worker logs expose failures; after edits, check the latest delivery record and public content. No CloudFront migration or alerting in this change. Production activation and expired-cache → origin-502 verification remain pending; the existing Cloudflare CLI login cannot access the site zone.
