Implemented workflow

The market data component collects each publisher independently and resolves archived prices by cutoff. Critical Elements ⚡️ and the on-demand forecast apply their own source policies. A conflict stops publication and carries the receipts an operator needs for manual investigation.

Before switching production consumers

  1. Review the production Terraform plan for replacement or deletion of existing storage and publication resources. Keep the current map publication running while collectors build the archive.
  2. Read four fully populated lag days through the normal forecast reader. Measure elapsed time and confirm that enough time remains for prediction, writing and retrieval before the issue deadline.
  3. Build a complete Critical Elements ⚡️ day from production receipts. Compare it with the served generation and explain any difference before switching publication.
  4. Witness one forecast issued and read before 11:00 Berlin. Retain its issue time, first observed availability, fit identity and price receipts.
