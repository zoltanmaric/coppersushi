# CNEC constraint selection — scope settled

The implemented product selects physical CNECs and Poland’s aggregate cap, with synchronized map/dropdown selection and interval navigation. Virtual-interface selection, rendering and attribution are removed; loss/ramping extensions are not planned for this map.

Current behavior belongs in [the app page](../copper-sushi-app.md#the-cnec-page). The [HTML explainer](../../assets/market-clearing.html) explains the country-spread decomposition and its distinction from counterfactual price effects.
