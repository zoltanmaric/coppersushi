# ACER electricity market integration monitoring report 2026

**Source:** ACER, *Progress of EU electricity wholesale market integration*, 4 November 2026, [report](https://www.acer.europa.eu/sites/default/files/documents/Publications/ACER-Monitoring-Report-electricity-market-integration-2026.pdf). Section 2.4.4, pp. 60–61, paragraphs 167–171 and Figure 43; paragraph 168 visually verified on 2026-11-13.

For the [Polish export-cap investigation](../polish-export-cap-investigation.md), the report establishes a material market effect: Polish import/export allocation constraints limited exchanges in 27% of 2025 market time units. Figure 43 gives 69%, 42%, 32% and 27% for 2022–2025; the 2022 sample begins with Core go-live in June. Paragraph 169 instead says 68% for 2022, an internal rounding/data discrepancy.

Paragraph 168 attributes the reserve rationale to PSE and says no balancing-capacity procurement precedes day-ahead clearing. This appears outdated: [URE’s December 2025 assessment](https://www.ure.gov.pl/en/communication/news/492,Assessment-of-the-impact-of-the-balancing-market-reform-on-the-application-of-al.html) describes pre-coupling procurement, and the [December 2025 explanatory document](core-da-ccm-fourth-amendment-explanatory-document.md) dates implementation to 14 June 2024 and the primary auction to 08:30 D−1. The investigation also retains actual pre-auction procurement publications.

The section does not independently validate individual Polish reserve calculations, separate import/export welfare effects, or compare more efficient alternatives. Its Nordic discussion identifies limited scrutiny of locally determined corridor limits. Other chapters cover wider integration and cross-zonal capacity monitoring.
