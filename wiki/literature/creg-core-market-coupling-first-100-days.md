# CREG: the first 100 days of Core market coupling

**Source:** Belgian Commission for Electricity and Gas Regulation (CREG), study F2458, approved 6 October 2022, [PDF](https://www.creg.be/sites/default/files/assets/Publications/Studies/F2458EN.pdf), executive summary, sections 3.2–3.3 and conclusion. Covers 9 June–16 September 2022; checked 2026-11-12.

CREG criticises structural use of [allocation constraints](../core-day-ahead-capacity-calculation.md#allocation-constraint), despite their permission under the methodology. It argues that Polish export restrictions reduced domestic prices at neighbours’ expense and compromised market efficiency and fairness. It calls for Elia and PSE to discontinue them. Its case study does not rerun the auction, so it does not quantify the full counterfactual welfare gain.

This is a regulator’s critical assessment of 2022, not proof that an individual 2026 limit is unjustified. Compare the [2026 safeguards](core-da-ccm-allocation-constraint-safeguards.md) and the [country-cap discussion](../country-import-export-caps.md#safeguards).
