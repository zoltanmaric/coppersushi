# SDAC allocation constraints, 2026

**Source:** Market Coupling TSOs, *Allocation Constraints in SDAC*, April 2026, [four-page PDF](https://eepublicdownloads.blob.core.windows.net/public-cdn-container/clean-documents/Network%20codes%20documents/NC%20CACM/SDAC%202026/Allocation_Constraints_in_SDAC.pdf). Read and visually checked page 3 on 2026-10-09.

## Scope

The Europe-wide inventory separates net-position caps from interconnector ramping and line-set constraints. Poland alone appears under net-position allocation constraints. A combined constraint covers Italy's exchanges with France, Austria and Slovenia. The cable section describes changes between intervals, not cable capacity ratings; its MW values must not be read as country-wide import/export caps.

The marked countries in the map are countries participating in SDAC, including offshore interconnectors. The coloured map is not a list of countries with aggregate caps. Intraday coupling has its own publications; this document concerns day-ahead coupling.

## Project-use

The [country-cap page](../country-import-export-caps.md#country-coverage) owns the app's per-country coverage and price attribution. Older Core handbooks describe cap mechanisms that do not all appear in this inventory. The inventory is an April 2026 publication, not proof that the rules cannot change afterward; the October audit supplements it with live and cached observations.
