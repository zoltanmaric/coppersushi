# Core allocation-constraint safeguards

**Source:** ACER Decision 10/2026, consolidated Core day-ahead capacity calculation methodology, 16 July 2026, [Annex II, official TSO copy](https://www.sepsas.sk/engine/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/ACER-Decision-10-2026-on-Fourth-amendment-of-the-Core-DA-CCM-Annex-II.pdf), Articles 7 and 27; Annex 1. Checked 2026-11-12.

Article 7(3) requires published underlying analysis. If an [allocation constraint](../core-day-ahead-capacity-calculation.md#allocation-constraint) has a non-zero shadow price in more than 0.1% of a quarter’s hours, its report must assess each affected interval’s economic-surplus loss, security effectiveness and alternatives. Applicable, more efficient alternatives must be implemented. Net-position caps are transitional until 8 June 2028; an amendment request by 8 December 2027 can extend the period pending a decision. Article 27 gives regulators compliance data and complete reports where public versions withhold sensitive material.

Annex 1 calculates Poland’s export bound from available generation less unavailable capacity, demand and upward reserves. It explicitly contemplates zero exports. This establishes the mechanism, not the accuracy of inputs for an individual interval.

See [country-cap analysis](../country-import-export-caps.md#safeguards) for the policy debate; the [proposal’s explanatory document](core-da-ccm-fourth-amendment-explanatory-document.md) is a separate source.

### Oversight

The [January 2025 Central Europe consultation report, response 12, printed p. 9](https://eepublicdownloads.entsoe.eu/clean-documents/nc-tasks/20250117%20CE%20DA%20CCM%20-%20Public%20Consultation%20Report.pdf) records a challenge to PSE’s evidence and analysis of alternatives. The TSOs respond that the reports are discussed at the Core Implementation Group, comprising TSOs, national regulators and ACER, and say there were no further objections. This is the TSOs’ account of review, not a published independent replication.

[URE’s December 2025 assessment](https://www.ure.gov.pl/download/9/15866/Ocenawplywureformyrynkubilansujacego.pdf), printed pp. 41–53, reviews activation using PSE data, assesses balancing reform and recommends additional measures. It documents notices to 823 generating-module owners in July–September 2025 over missing schedules, and requests stronger penalty powers. This demonstrates action on a contributor to reserve uncertainty, not verification of every individual cap. Recommendations include broader reserve access, qualification changes, scheduling and balancing incentives.

As checked on 2026-11-12, the public sources reviewed establish reporting, institutional discussion and regulator recommendations. They do not establish an independently reproduced per-interval necessity test or completion of every more efficient alternative. The [Q4 2025 assessment](pse-allocation-constraints-q4-2025.md) is evidence of an actual counterfactual calculation, with narrower scope than comparison against an improved market design.
